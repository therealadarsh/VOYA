What the software stores today

  • A hash of the account email and a hash of the session token
  • Trip requests: destination, dates, budget, party size, interests
  • Spend amounts, category, day, and a short note
  • Opt-in memory keys: interests, pace, tip percent, firmness, adults
  • Feedback messages you submit
  • Magic-link tokens until they are used or they expire

The software does not ask for income. Stored coordinates use at most 3 decimal places.
